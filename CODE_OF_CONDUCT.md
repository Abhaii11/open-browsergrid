# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

OpenBrowserGrid is committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone—regardless of background, experience level, identity, or affiliation.

### Expected Behavior
- Be constructive, patient, and welcoming to newcomers and fellow contributors.
- Focus feedback on code, architecture, and trade-offs rather than individuals.
- Respect differing viewpoints and collaborate in good faith.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

### Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Any form of discriminatory, abusive, or exclusionary conduct.

## Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies across all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and official community channels.

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub or at `rautabhay66@gmail.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may warn, remove content from, or ban participants who violate these standards.

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (version 2.1).
